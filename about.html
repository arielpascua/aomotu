What we are

Company Background

AOMOTU is a dynamic agency where digital imagination meets real world outcomes. We at AOMOTU are dedicated to innovation, sustainability and empowerment. We advocate ethical standards, wellness-oriented solutions and collaborative approaches to help companies thrive in competitive marketplaces. We develop ideas into powerful brands that engage with people and generate measurable results through online campaigns, printed materials, gifts and product distribution.

Our backbone structure consists of robust manufacturing, offset printing, technology and trade activities. Through this integrated approach, we are able to create end-to-end solutions that carry businesses from concept to customer seamlessly.

AOMOTU is a multi-service agency that bridges the gap between digital creativity and physical brand execution. It offers end-to-end solutions combining digital advertising, marketing strategy, outsourcing operations, manufacturing, offset printing, corporate giveaways, and trading — all under one unified ecosystem.

03
What we set out to do

Our Purpose

By integrating technology, design, and production, AOMOTU enables businesses to scale efficiently, build stronger brand identities, and deliver consistent experiences across both online and offline channels.

  • We provide comprehensive advertising, marketing, and outsourcing services that combine digital innovation with product development to support brand growth.
  • Support product development and distribution by leveraging the manufacturing arm, which produces skincare, cosmetics, fragrances, household essentials, cleaning products, and sanitation solutions, beverages, supplements, and color cosmetics.
  • Enhance brand visibility through offset printing (Labels and Packaging) and corporate giveaways to reinforce identity and engage customers.
  • Support market expansion by serving as a trading company for brands from the manufacturing division, enabling smooth entry into various markets.
  • Promote sustainability and wellness by ensuring product innovation aligns with ethical practices and supports consumer well-being.

Where we are going

Vision & Mission

Vision

We aspire to ignite a global movement as the premier hub for integrated advertising, marketing, and outsourcing services. At AOMOTU, we spark transformation, turning ideas into iconic brands and delivering sustainable results that elevate solutions that drive business growth. Our passion fuels innovation and wellness keeps us at the forefront of global markets.

Mission

At AOMOTU, our mission is to:

  • Elevate brands by combining digital advertising expertise with manufacturing and trading capabilities.
  • Deliver holistic solutions that integrate offset printing, corporate giveaways, and omnichannel marketing strategies.
  • Support product innovation and distribution across skincare, cosmetics, color cosmetics, fragrance and perfumery, household products, cleaning and janitorial solutions, and alcohol and sanitation essentials.
  • Strengthen identity and visibility through creative campaigns and tangible brand assets.
  • Champion sustainability and wellness by aligning ethical practices with consumer needs.
  • Drive measurable growth by acting as a transformational partner for businesses seeking scalability, efficiency, and long-term success.

Founded 2023

Our Story

AOMOTU Inc. is originally founded by the Mastermind “Jamaica Base Escolano” — “Jam” — on a principle of distributed intelligence. She conceptualized a one stop shop to build bespoke marketing pipelines and localized media hubs. Guided by her vision, “Jam” started off collaborating ideas and concepts with her sister to maximize a global concept and materialize digital concept. Year 2023 she officially dropped an ecosystem of a powerhouse which has a complete setup of branding; as formulator, artist and geologist by profession, she materialized the tradition and her sister the digi-works that meets the outsourcing, digital solutions into innovation.

AOMOTU, as “Jam” integrates a unique operational model which has borderless digital solutions, continues building the empire. She builds with the people she incorporates her mission, vision, goals and values.

Now, we span 16 countries as a limitless and borderless one-stop shop ecosystem globally.
