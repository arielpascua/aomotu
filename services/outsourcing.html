01

Customer Support

  • Omnichannel Support Managing customer inquiries via email, live chat, ticketing systems (e.g. Zendesk, Freshdesk), and social media DMs.
  • Voice Support Inbound and outbound calls, technical troubleshooting, and order status updates.
  • Quality Assurance Monitoring interactions to ensure adherence to brand voice and service standards.
  • Reporting Weekly and monthly dashboards on ticket volume, response time, and customer satisfaction (CSAT) scores.
02

Back-Office Operations

  • Data Management Data entry, database maintenance, and data cleansing.
  • Financial Processing Accounts payable and receivable, payroll processing, and expense tracking.
  • Order Fulfillment Inventory management, order processing, and shipment coordination.
  • Transcription & Documentation Converting audio and video meetings into written records and organizing company files.
03

IT & Cloud Solutions

  • Cloud Infrastructure Setting up and managing cloud environments (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure) and cloud storage migration.
  • Technical Support Help-desk support for internal employees (password resets, software troubleshooting).
  • Cybersecurity Monitoring system threats, managing backups, and ensuring data privacy compliance.
  • Software Maintenance Regular software updates, patch management, and server monitoring to ensure 99.9% uptime.
04

Virtual Assistance

  • Administrative Support Calendar management, email inbox organization, and travel arrangements.
  • Project Coordination Task tracking, meeting minute taking, and follow-up reminders.
  • Lead Generation Researching potential clients, managing CRM entries, and organizing outreach lists.
  • Personal Assistance Specialized research, document preparation, and recurring task management.
