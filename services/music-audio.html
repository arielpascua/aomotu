Music & Audio

01

Jingle

  • Creative Composition Developing original melodies, hooks, and lyrical concepts tailored to the brand's identity.
  • Arrangement Scoring instruments, beats, and backing tracks to match the desired mood — upbeat, corporate, emotional.
  • Sonic Branding Creating a musical logo or short audio mnemonic that makes the brand instantly recognizable.
02

Recording (per hour)

  • Studio Time Access to a soundproof recording environment equipped with professional microphones, pre-amps, and monitoring gear.
  • Engineering On-site audio technician to manage gain staging, microphone placement, and track organization.
  • Session Management Directing the talent, managing take logs, and ensuring all raw audio files are captured at professional standards (24-bit/48kHz).
03

Voice-over

  • Casting Sourcing and auditioning professional voice talent — accents, tone, age ranges — to fit the project.
  • Direction Coaching the talent on pacing, emphasis, and emotional delivery to ensure the script resonates with the target audience.
  • File Clean-up Removing breath noise, clicks, and background hums from the raw vocal tracks.
04

Mixing

  • Level Balancing Adjusting the volume of individual elements — vocals, instruments, SFX — so they sit perfectly in the final track.
  • Frequency Management (EQ & Compression) Using industry-standard processing to ensure clarity, warmth, and professional impact.
  • Spatial Effects Adding reverb, delay, and stereo widening to create depth and professional polish.
  • Mastering Final limiting and volume normalization to ensure the audio meets loudness standards for TV, radio, or streaming (Spotify/YouTube).
05

Original Music

  • Custom Scoring Composing background music for videos, ads, or presentations that avoids copyright issues (royalty-free or exclusive rights).
  • Instrumentation Sourcing or recording live instruments or high-quality virtual instrument libraries.
  • Sync Rights Clearing and assigning full usage rights for the client to use the music across various media platforms.
06

Talent Coordination

  • Booking Negotiating rates, contracts, and schedules for singers, voice actors, or session musicians.
  • Logistics Managing studio arrival times, refreshment needs, and technical riders.
  • Rights & Releases Managing legal paperwork, talent releases, and usage agreements to ensure the client holds the rights to the performance.
