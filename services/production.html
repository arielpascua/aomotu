01

TV Commercial

  • Production Planning Creative concept development, scriptwriting, and storyboard creation.
  • Pre-Production Casting talent, location scouting, art direction, and production scheduling.
  • Full-Scale Execution Multi-camera setup, professional lighting, sound design, and on-set directing.
  • Deliverables Master files in broadcast-ready formats (HD/4K, various aspect ratios) and compliance with TV ad specifications.
02

Digital Ad Production

  • Platform Optimization Aspect ratio adaptation — 9:16 for Reels and TikTok, 1:1 for feed, 16:9 for pre-roll.
  • Fast-Paced Editing Creating hook-driven content designed to capture attention in the first 3 seconds.
  • Dynamic Assets Motion graphics, text overlays, and subtitles integration.
  • A/B Testing Versions Producing multiple iterations, such as different CTAs or hooks, for performance marketing.
03

Corporate Video

  • Narrative Development Interviews with leadership, company culture storytelling, and testimonial gathering.
  • Professional Cinematography High-quality lighting and composition that reflects a professional corporate image.
  • Audio / Voiceover High-fidelity microphone setups for interviews and professional voiceover recording.
  • B-Roll Capture Detailed shots of office environments, team interactions, and facility operations.
04

Product Video

  • Macro / Detail Shots Close-up product cinematography to showcase texture, quality, and features.
  • Lifestyle Integration Staging the product in real-world environments to show use-cases.
  • Product Motion Graphics Adding specifications, pricing, or feature labels as animations over the video.
  • 360-Degree Views Creating seamless rotation shots for e-commerce catalog use.
05

Photography (per day)

  • On-Set Production Full-day photography coverage — portraits, lifestyle, or commercial.
  • Professional Lighting Use of studio strobes or LED continuous lighting rigs.
  • Post-Production / Retouching Standard color correction, white balancing, and professional skin or product retouching.
  • Deliverables High-resolution digital files (RAW plus JPG/TIFF) for both print and digital use.
06

Videography (per day)

  • On-Set Operation Skilled camera operator for standard b-roll, event footage, or interview capture.
  • Equipment Package Standard camera, lens, tripod, and basic on-camera audio.
  • Field Management Efficient setup and teardown for rapid-turnaround shoots.
07

Multi-camera

  • Live Vision Switching Using a switcher (such as an ATEM) to record or broadcast from multiple angles simultaneously.
  • Synchronization Timecode syncing for seamless editing across multiple camera sources.
  • Complex Coverage Covering large interviews, panels, conferences, or multi-angle product demos.
  • Audio Routing Managing inputs from multiple microphones into a centralized mixing board.
08

Drone

  • Regulatory Compliance Flight planning, airspace analysis, and securing necessary permits and clearances.
  • Aerial Cinematography Capturing high-altitude wide shots, tracking shots, and architectural reveals.
  • Safety Protocols Deployment of spotters and site risk assessments to ensure safety.
09

Studio Coordination

  • Logistics Management Booking studio space, managing catering, and handling talent and crew needs.
  • Prop & Set Management Sourcing or building set elements, managing background assets, and wardrobe coordination.
  • On-Site Liaison Serving as the central point of contact between the client and the creative crew to ensure project requirements are met.
10

Post-production

  • Editorial Assembly Pacing, rhythmic editing, and narrative structure.
  • Cinematic Color Grading Adjusting color temperature, contrast, and saturation to establish a specific look and mood.
  • Sound Engineering Audio cleanup, background noise removal, music sourcing and licensing, and sound effects layering.
  • Motion Graphics / VFX Logo animation, lower-thirds, kinetic typography, and basic visual effects.
