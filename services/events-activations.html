01

Event Management

  • End-to-End Planning Budget management, timeline creation, and vendor coordination across caterers, security, and venues.
  • Site Management On-site supervision, floor plan design, and safety and compliance management.
  • Post-Event Reporting Debriefing, attendee analytics, and budget reconciliation.
02

Brand Activation

  • Concept Development Creative ideas that bring brand values to life.
  • Installation Design Fabrication of physical structures, interactive kiosks, or photo booths.
  • Consumer Engagement Developing interactive activities — games, sampling, demos — to encourage brand interaction.
03

Experiential Marketing

  • Immersion Strategy Designing sensory experiences (lighting, sound, smell, tactile) that leave a lasting memory.
  • Digital Integration Utilizing AR/VR, social media integration, or touch-screen tech to enhance the visitor experience.
  • Guerrilla Marketing Creating high-impact, non-traditional pop-up experiences in high-traffic areas.
04

Product Launch

  • Theatrical Production Stage design, lighting design, AV production, and scriptwriting for the reveal.
  • Influencer & Media Management Inviting key press and influencers, managing press kits, and handling red-carpet coordination.
  • Content Production Creating launch-day hype videos, press release materials, and social media teasers.
05

Corporate Event

  • Gala / Dinner Planning Menu curation, decor, entertainment booking, and seating plans.
  • Award Ceremony Management Scripting, teleprompter services, and award and trophy procurement.
  • VIP Handling Managing executive-level accommodations, transportation, and special amenities.
06

Conference

  • Content & Speaker Management Managing speaker briefings, presentation slides (PowerPoint/Keynote), and Q&A facilitation.
  • Registration Systems Managing digital ticketing, badge printing, and attendee check-in software.
  • Technical Support Ensuring seamless audio-visual transitions, high-speed Wi-Fi, and live streaming capabilities.
07

Roadshow

  • Logistics & Routing Managing multiple locations, city-to-city transportation, and site setup and teardown schedules.
  • Uniformity Ensuring the brand look and feel is identical across every city and location.
  • Local Permitting Coordinating with local authorities for street-level activations or venue bookings.
08

Trade Show

  • Booth Design & Fabrication Designing custom modular booths that fit specific expo dimensions.
  • Collateral Production Printing brochures, flyers, and business cards for attendee distribution.
  • Lead Generation Strategy Implementing digital scan-to-capture systems to collect visitor data.
09

Event Staffing

  • Role Profiling Identifying the right staff — hosts and hostesses, brand ambassadors, technical crew, security.
  • Training & Briefing Hosting pre-event workshops so staff understand the brand, the product, and their specific responsibilities.
  • Uniform Management Sourcing, branding, and coordinating event attire.
10

Manpower (per day)

  • Operational Staffing Billed per person per shift — set-up crew, ushers, registration staff.
  • Performance Monitoring Ensuring staff performance, attendance, and adherence to house rules.
  • On-Call Logistics Managing substitute personnel if last-minute absences occur.
