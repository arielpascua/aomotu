01

Creative Strategy & Campaign Development

  • Market research, target audience persona mapping, campaign conceptualization, and creative direction strategy.
02

Branding & Visual Identity

  • Development of the brand's look and feel, including mood boards, color palette theory, typography systems, and brand positioning statements.
03

Logo Design

  • Primary logo design, alternate versions (stacked, horizontal, icon-only), and favicon or app icon variations.
04

Brand Guidelines

  • A comprehensive brand bible covering logo usage, color codes (CMYK/RGB/HEX), font hierarchy, imagery style, and do's and don'ts.
05

Graphic Design (per layout)

  • Design of marketing collaterals including brochures, flyers, white papers, newsletters, business cards, and print advertisements.
06

Key Visual Design

  • Creation of the hero creative asset that establishes the visual anchor for a specific campaign, adaptable across all media.
07

Copywriting

  • Crafting persuasive headlines, body copy, taglines, scripts for videos, and website landing page content.
08

Social Media Content (Monthly)

  • Content calendar planning, post design, caption writing, and hashtag strategy — for example, a package that includes 12 posts per month.
09

Motion Graphics

  • Creation of animated logos, kinetic typography, and graphic transitions for social media and advertisements.
10

Video Editing

  • Professional post-production, including color grading, audio mixing, subtitle integration, and pacing for platforms like Reels, TikTok, or YouTube.
11

Presentation / Pitch Deck

  • Professional slide design for investor pitches, company profiles, or sales decks, including custom iconography and data visualization.
