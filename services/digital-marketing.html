Social Media Campaigns

  • Strategy & Planning Social media audit, audience targeting, and content calendar development.
  • Account Management Profile optimization, daily posting, and platform maintenance (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok).
  • Community Management Responding to comments and DMs, and engaging with followers.

SEO / SEM

  • SEO Audit & Optimization Website technical audit, keyword research, and on-page optimization (titles, meta descriptions, alt text).
  • Content SEO Optimizing existing web pages for higher search ranking.
  • Off-Page SEO Backlink strategy and local directory submissions.
  • SEM (Pay-Per-Click) Google Ads account setup, keyword bidding strategy, ad copy writing, and landing page conversion tracking.
  • Performance Tracking Monthly traffic analytics, ranking reports, and search volume insights.

Content Creation

  • Written Content Blog articles, newsletter copy, website landing page copy, and professional scripts.
  • Graphic Design Social media post visuals, infographics, email banners, and ad creative assets.
  • Video Production Short-form video editing (Reels/TikTok/Shorts), promotional video creation, and basic motion graphics.
  • Content Calendar Monthly editorial planning and scheduling.

Brand Strategy
