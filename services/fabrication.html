01

Booth Design

  • Space Planning Floor plan development, traffic flow analysis, and layout optimization for maximum engagement.
  • 3D Rendering Creating high-fidelity 3D visualizations to help the client walk through the booth before production.
  • Branding Integration Mapping logos, colors, and key messages onto the structure for maximum brand impact.
02

Booth Fabrication

  • Custom Build Constructing counters, display walls, shelving, and cabinets to meet specific dimensions.
  • Material Sourcing Selection of high-quality substrates — MDF, aluminum framing, plexiglass and similar.
  • Hardware Integration Installing specialized hardware such as locks, hinges, monitor mounts, and power ports.
03

Stage Construction

  • Structural Planning Load-bearing calculations, safety inspections, and site compliance checks.
  • Assembly Professional staging build-out — risers, decks, staircases, ramps — to accommodate presenters and equipment.
  • Finishing Applying skirting, carpeting, or flooring overlays to match the event's theme.
04

Exhibit Fabrication

  • Interactive Displays Building custom touch-screens, prop displays, or product-specific viewing stations.
  • Modular Component Assembly Pre-assembling display units that are easy to transport and quick to set up on-site.
  • Detailed Finishes Paint, vinyl wraps, and specialized lighting fixtures integrated directly into the display units.
05

Signage

  • Directional / Wayfinding Designing and printing signs to help attendees navigate the venue.
  • Promotional Signage Creating foam-core, sintra-board, or acrylic displays for announcements and branding.
  • Mounting / Installation Professional site installation using hanging hardware, floor stands, or wall adhesives.
06

Large Format Printing

  • Materials High-resolution printing on tarpaulin, vinyl, canvas, or back-lit film for lightboxes.
  • Production Large-scale printing for backdrops, floor decals, wall murals, and hanging banners.
  • Finishing Hemming, eyelet placement, pole pockets, or UV lamination for weather and scratch protection.
07

Event Styling

  • Conceptualization Developing a look and feel — minimalist, rustic, luxury, futuristic.
  • Prop Curation Sourcing decorative elements, furniture, plants, and art installations.
  • Layout & Arrangement Art-directing the placement of furniture and decor on-site for the perfect aesthetic.
08

Lights & Sounds

  • Lighting Design Setup of stage wash, spotlights, mood lighting, and moving heads (intelligent lights).
  • Sound Engineering Providing professional PA systems, wireless microphones, mixers, and audio monitoring for clear sound output.
  • Tech Management Dedicated sound and light engineers to operate equipment throughout the event.
09

LED Wall (per day)

  • Hardware Rental Providing high-resolution indoor and outdoor LED panels (P2, P3, P4 pixel pitches).
  • Content Deployment Professional technicians to map and play the client's media — videos, presentations, live feeds.
  • Maintenance On-site support to monitor for technical issues, power supply, and signal stability throughout the event.
