01

Talent Sourcing

  • Database Access Utilizing professional networks to identify talent that fits specific demographic and aesthetic requirements.
  • Vetting Background checks, social sentiment analysis, and verifying engagement authenticity by checking for fake followers and bots.
  • Availability Screening Handling the initial outreach to check schedules and interest levels before presenting a shortlist to the client.
02

Celebrity Booking

  • Representation Liaison Direct negotiation with agents, managers, and publicists to secure celebrities for appearances or endorsements.
  • Contract Management Handling complex legal riders, payment schedules, and usage rights — where, when, and how long the celebrity's image can be used.
  • Logistics & Rider Fulfillment Managing celebrity rider requirements: travel, accommodation, catering, security, and hair and makeup.
03

Influencer Campaign

  • Strategy & Briefing Defining the campaign goal — awareness versus conversion — and developing detailed creative briefs for the influencers.
  • Campaign Rollout Overseeing the content production timeline, from initial draft submission to final posting.
  • Tracking & Optimization Real-time monitoring of campaign performance (clicks, engagement) and adjusting strategy mid-campaign if needed.
04

KOL Management

  • Long-term Relationship Building Nurturing relationships with industry experts and niche authorities for ongoing brand ambassadorships.
  • Performance Auditing Providing deep-dive analytics on how well the KOL's audience responds to specific messages or product claims.
  • Community Integration Coordinating KOL appearances at industry events, webinars, or as featured experts in company content.
05

Talent Casting

  • Audition Management Organizing and managing casting calls, in-person or digital, for actors, models, or spokespeople.
  • Shortlisting Providing high-quality video and photo reels and headshots to the client for final selection.
  • Contractual Execution Finalizing the service agreements and performance releases for the selected cast members.
