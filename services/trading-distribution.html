Division 05

Trading & Distribution

Customs, freight, warehousing, retail placement and supply chain planning that move goods to market.

4 services · 16 capabilities

Request a quote
01

Import / Export

  • Customs Brokerage Managing documentation (Bill of Lading, commercial invoices, packing lists) to ensure smooth clearance through customs.
  • Trade Compliance Ensuring all goods meet local and international regulations, including tariff classification and tax computation.
  • Documentation Support Preparation of Certificates of Origin, import and export licenses, and other government-mandated permits.
  • Freight Forwarding Liaison Coordinating with international carriers to secure competitive rates for sea, air, or land freight.
02

Logistics

  • Warehousing & Storage Secure facility management, including climate-controlled storage for sensitive goods and inventory tracking.
  • Last-Mile Delivery Managing the final leg of the journey to ensure goods arrive safely at the client's facility or retail location.
  • Distribution Routing Planning efficient delivery routes to minimize costs and ensure timely delivery across various regions.
  • Order Fulfillment Picking, packing, and labeling products according to specific client requirements before dispatch.
03

Retail Partnerships

  • Channel Management Acting as the primary point of contact between the manufacturer and retail chains (supermarkets, department stores, e-commerce platforms).
  • Merchandising Support Assisting with shelf-stocking, promotional displays, and product placement strategies to maximize retail sell-through.
  • Negotiation & Placement Assisting in securing shelf space, listing agreements, and coordinating seasonal promotions with retail buyers.
  • Retail Reporting Providing insights on sell-through rates, inventory turnover, and market feedback from retail outlets.
04

Supply Chain Management

  • Demand Planning Forecasting market needs to ensure the client maintains optimal stock levels, preventing both overstock and stockouts.
  • Supplier Relationship Management Auditing and managing third-party suppliers to ensure consistency in quality and lead times.
  • Inventory Auditing Regular physical counts and reconciliations to ensure digital records match actual on-hand stock.
  • Supply Chain Optimization Identifying bottlenecks in the process to reduce lead times and total landed costs for the client.
