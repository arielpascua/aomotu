Corporate Giveaways

Promotional items, executive gift sets, and event merchandise — sourced, branded, kitted, and shipped.

3 services · 11 capabilities

In this division

Request a quote
01

Custom Promotional Items

  • Product Sourcing Curating a catalog of high-utility items (e.g. tech gadgets, stationery, drinkware, apparel) that match the client's industry and budget.
  • Custom Branding (Imprinting) Applying logos and brand colors using screen or pad printing for plastic, fabric and metal items; laser engraving for a premium, permanent finish on metal, wood or leather; embroidery for high-quality apparel and bags; and full-color digital printing for items requiring complex graphics or photographic detail.
  • Sample Prototyping Providing physical pre-production samples so the client can approve the quality and logo placement before the bulk order is processed.
02

Branded Gifts

  • Gift Curation (Themed Sets) Designing cohesive gift kits, such as welcome kits for new hires or executive gift boxes for VIP clients.
  • Premium Packaging & Assembly Providing high-end packaging options — matte-finish gift boxes, custom ribbons, magnetic-closure boxes, and branded tissue paper — to enhance the unboxing experience.
  • Personalization Adding individual recipient names to items (e.g. laser-engraved names on tumblers or leather notebooks) to create a high-value, personal feel.
  • Direct-to-Recipient Shipping Managing the logistics of shipping individual gift sets to multiple home or office addresses globally.
03

Event Merchandise

  • Volume Planning Providing tiered pricing for large-scale production runs to ensure cost-efficiency for major conferences, trade shows, or corporate galas.
  • Kitting & Collating Assembling all event-related items (lanyard, badge, pen, notebook, branded shirt) into a single, ready-to-distribute bag.
  • On-Site Fulfillment Prep Ensuring all merchandise is organized, checked for quality, and packed in labeled containers for easy handling at the event venue.
  • Sustainable Swag Options Sourcing eco-friendly alternatives (bamboo, recycled cotton, post-consumer plastics) to align with the client's CSR goals.
