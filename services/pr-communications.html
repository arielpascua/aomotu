PR & Communications

01

PR Retainer

  • Strategic Counsel Monthly meetings to align PR efforts with business goals and market trends.
  • Monitoring & Reporting Tracking media sentiment, brand mentions, and providing a monthly analysis report of reach and impact.
  • Reputation Management Proactive monitoring of online discussions to maintain a positive brand image.
  • Media List Maintenance Constantly updating and cultivating the database of journalists, influencers, and industry contacts.
02

Media Relations

  • Pitching Proactively reaching out to editors, journalists, and producers to secure media placements.
  • Relationship Management Hosting informal meetups, building rapport, and positioning the client as an industry expert to media gatekeepers.
  • Interview Preparation Preparing briefing documents, talking points, and Q&A prep for clients before media appearances.
  • Desk-side Briefings Coordinating one-on-one meetings between clients and key target media outlets.
03

Press Release

  • Drafting & Content Writing news-worthy, AP-style press releases that capture interest and adhere to industry standards.
  • Distribution Pushing content to wire services (such as PR Newswire and local business journals) and targeted distribution lists.
  • Multimedia Integration Enhancing releases with high-quality images, infographics, or video links to increase pick-up rates.
  • Follow-up Calling and emailing journalists post-distribution to track status and pitch angles for follow-up stories.
04

Press Event

  • Media Invitations & RSVP Creating target media lists, managing secure invitations, and handling RSVP tracking.
  • On-site Press Management Running a dedicated press desk or media area, handing out press kits, and managing photo and video opportunities.
  • Production Coordinating the event logistics — AV, lighting, venue — specifically to optimize for media coverage.
  • Post-Event Follow-up Distributing event photos, transcripts, and thank-you notes to all attendees to encourage coverage.
05

PR Campaign

  • Campaign Strategy Defining the hook, identifying the target audience, and selecting the right media mix (traditional versus digital).
  • Asset Creation Developing specialized campaign themes, landing pages, and creative PR collateral.
  • Launch Timeline Managing a multi-channel rollout — teasers, embargoed releases, launch day, and follow-up stories.
  • Outcome Evaluation Measuring success against pre-defined KPIs such as number of placements, audience reach, and estimated ad value.
06

Crisis Communications

  • Vulnerability Assessment Identifying potential risks to the brand's reputation before they become emergencies.
  • Crisis Playbook Developing standardized holding statements, internal FAQs, and communication protocols for various scenarios.
  • Active Response Serving as the direct spokesperson or advising leadership in real-time during a public incident.
  • Post-Crisis Recovery Implementing a long-term strategy to rebuild trust and neutralize negative press coverage.
