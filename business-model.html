Eight building blocks

Our Business Model

01

Customer Segments

  • Retail & E-commerce brands needing digital campaigns and packaging.
  • Corporate clients requiring giveaways, merchandising, and event branding.
  • SMEs & Startups outsourcing operations for scalability.
  • Manufacturers & distributors seeking trading and logistics support.
  • Real estate & construction firms needing marketing and printed materials.
02

Value Proposition

  • End-to-End Integration: Digital + outsourcing + manufacturing + trading under one roof.
  • Brand Consistency: Unified execution across online and offline touchpoints.
  • Cost Efficiency: Streamlined supply chain and outsourcing solutions.
  • Scalability: Flexible outsourcing and production capacity for growth.
03

Channels

  • Direct sales through corporate partnerships.
  • Digital platforms (website, social media, e-commerce).
  • Trade shows, expos, and industry events.
  • Distribution networks and retail collaborations.
04

Customer Relationships

  • Long-term contracts with corporate clients.
  • Project-based engagements for campaigns and events.
  • Personalized account management and outsourcing support.
  • Loyalty programs for repeat clients (giveaways, printing, trading).
05

Revenue Streams

  • Digital advertising retainers and campaign fees.
  • Outsourcing service contracts (customer service, IT, back-office).
  • Manufacturing and printing orders (packaging, brochures, branded materials).
  • Corporate giveaways and merchandising sales.
  • Trading margins from import/export and distribution.
06

Key Resources

  • Skilled creative and marketing teams.
  • Outsourcing workforce and IT infrastructure.
  • Manufacturing and printing facilities.
  • Supplier and trading networks.
  • Brand partnerships and client relationships.
07

Key Activities

  • Designing and executing digital campaigns.
  • Managing outsourcing operations.
  • Manufacturing and printing branded assets.
  • Producing and sourcing corporate giveaways.
  • Trading, logistics, and distribution management.
08

Key Partnerships

  • Social media and ad-tech platforms.
  • BPO and IT service providers.
  • Printing and packaging suppliers.
  • Merchandise and promotional product vendors.
  • Logistics and trading partners.
  • Industry associations and sustainability organizations.
