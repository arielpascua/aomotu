Our story

AOMOTU Inc. was founded by Jamaica Base Escolano — "Jam" — on a principle of distributed intelligence: a one-stop shop for bespoke marketing pipelines and localised media hubs.

She built it out with her sister, who carried the digital and outsourcing side. A formulator, artist and geologist by profession, Jam brought the tradition; her sister brought the digital. In 2023 the ecosystem launched with a full branding setup.

The operating model is borderless by design, and it is still built with the people who carry its mission and values.

Now we span 16 countries as a limitless and borderless one-stop-shop ecosystem, globally.

SEO / SEM

Content Creation

Manufacturing & Offset Printing

High-Quality Printing · Packaging Design · Large-scale Production
High-Quality Printing
  • Commercial Offset Printing: high-volume production of brochures, catalogues, magazines, and business stationery with precise colour matching (Pantone/CMYK).
  • Specialised Finishes: spot UV, foil stamping, embossing/debossing, matte/gloss lamination, and die-cutting.
  • Pre-press Services: file verification, colour proofing, impositioning, and layout adjustments to ensure error-free print runs.
  • Variable Data Printing (VDP): personalised direct mail, numbered tickets, or custom certificates produced in a single print run.
Packaging Design
  • Structural Engineering: designing custom box shapes, folding cartons, and displays that are structurally sound and cost-effective to ship.
  • Graphic Design & Branding: creating eye-catching visuals, typography, and layout that align with the client's brand identity for retail shelves.
  • Prototyping & Mock-ups: creating physical 3D samples for client approval before full-scale production, to test fit, form, and aesthetics.
  • Regulatory Compliance: designing layouts that correctly include necessary labelling requirements (barcodes, nutritional info, safety warnings).
Large-scale Production
  • Bulk Run Management: high-speed, automated production cycles designed for large-volume retail or corporate needs.
  • Quality Control Protocols: rigorous inspections during the production cycle — checkpoints for colour consistency, glue strength, and folding accuracy.
  • Inventory & Warehousing: storing bulk orders and offering "release on demand" shipping to help clients manage physical storage space.
  • Logistical Fulfilment: efficient kitting, assembly, and distribution of finished goods to multiple locations or distribution centres.

Corporate Giveaways

Promotional Items · Branded Gifts · Event Merchandise
Custom Promotional Items
  • Product Sourcing: curating a catalogue of high-utility items (tech gadgets, stationery, drinkware, apparel) matched to the client's industry and budget.
  • Custom Branding (Imprinting): screen/pad printing for plastic, fabric or metal; laser engraving for a premium permanent finish on metal, wood or leather; embroidery for apparel and bags; full-colour digital printing for complex graphics.
  • Sample Prototyping: physical pre-production samples so the client can approve quality and logo placement before the bulk order is processed.
Branded Gifts
  • Gift Curation (Themed Sets): cohesive gift kits such as welcome kits for new hires and executive gift boxes for VIP clients.
  • Premium Packaging & Assembly: matte-finish gift boxes, custom ribbons, magnetic-closure boxes, and branded tissue paper to enhance the unboxing experience.
  • Personalisation: adding individual recipient names to items — laser-engraved names on tumblers or leather notebooks — for a high-value, personal feel.
  • Direct-to-Recipient Shipping: managing the logistics of shipping individual gift sets to multiple home or office addresses globally.
Event Merchandise
  • Volume Planning: tiered pricing for large-scale production runs, ensuring cost-efficiency for major conferences, trade shows or corporate galas.
  • Kitting & Collating: assembling all event items (lanyard, badge, pen, notebook, branded shirt) into a single ready-to-distribute bag.
  • On-Site Fulfilment Prep: merchandise organised, quality-checked and packed in labelled containers for easy handling at the venue.
  • Sustainable Swag Options: eco-friendly alternatives (bamboo, recycled cotton, post-consumer plastics) aligned to the client's CSR goals.

Trading and Distribution

Import/Export · Logistics · Retail · Supply Chain
Import / Export
  • Customs Brokerage: managing documentation (bill of lading, commercial invoices, packing lists) to ensure smooth clearance through customs.
  • Trade Compliance: ensuring all goods meet local and international regulations, including tariff classification and tax computation.
  • Documentation Support: preparation of certificates of origin, import/export licences, and other government-mandated permits.
  • Freight Forwarding Liaison: coordinating with international carriers to secure competitive rates for sea, air or land freight.
Logistics
  • Warehousing & Storage: secure facility management, including climate-controlled storage for sensitive goods and inventory tracking.
  • Last-Mile Delivery: managing the final leg to ensure goods arrive safely at the client's facility or retail location.
  • Distribution Routing: planning efficient delivery routes to minimise costs and ensure timely delivery across regions.
  • Order Fulfilment: picking, packing and labelling products according to specific client requirements before dispatch.
Retail Partnerships
  • Channel Management: acting as the primary point of contact between the manufacturer and retail chains (supermarkets, department stores, e-commerce platforms).
  • Merchandising Support: assisting with shelf-stocking, promotional displays, and product placement strategies to maximise retail sell-through.
  • Negotiation & Placement: securing shelf space, listing agreements, and coordinating seasonal promotions with retail buyers.
  • Retail Reporting: insights on sell-through rates, inventory turnover, and market feedback from retail outlets.
Supply Chain Management
  • Demand Planning: forecasting market needs so the client maintains optimal stock levels, preventing both overstock and stockouts.
  • Supplier Relationship Management: auditing and managing third-party suppliers to ensure consistency in quality and lead times.
  • Inventory Auditing: regular physical counts and reconciliations so digital records match actual on-hand stock.
  • Supply Chain Optimisation: identifying bottlenecks to reduce lead times and total landed costs.

Creative & Digital

Campaign Strategy · Identity · Key Visuals · Copywriting
  • Creative Strategy & Campaign Development: market research, target audience persona mapping, campaign conceptualisation, and creative direction strategy.
  • Branding & Visual Identity: development of the brand's look and feel, including mood boards, colour palette theory, typography systems, and brand positioning statements.
  • Logo Design: primary logo design, alternate versions (stacked, horizontal, icon-only), and favicon/app icon variations.
  • Brand Guidelines: a comprehensive brand bible covering logo usage, colour codes (CMYK/RGB/HEX), font hierarchy, imagery style, and do's and don'ts.
  • Graphic Design (per layout): marketing collaterals including brochures, flyers, white papers, newsletters, business cards, and print advertisements.
  • Key Visual Design: the hero creative asset that establishes the visual anchor for a campaign, adaptable across all media.
  • Copywriting: persuasive headlines, body copy, taglines, video scripts, and website landing page content.
  • Social Media Content (monthly): content calendar planning, post design, caption writing, and hashtag strategy — e.g. a package of 12 posts per month.

Production

TVC · Digital Ads · Video · Photography · Post-production
TV Commercial
  • Production Planning: creative concept development, scriptwriting, and storyboard creation.
  • Pre-Production: casting talent, location scouting, art direction, and production scheduling.
  • Full-Scale Execution: multi-camera setup, professional lighting, sound design, and on-set directing.
  • Deliverables: master files in broadcast-ready formats (HD/4K, various aspect ratios) compliant with TV ad specifications.
Digital Ad Production
  • Platform Optimisation: aspect ratio adaptation (9:16 for Reels/TikTok, 1:1 for feed, 16:9 for pre-roll).
  • Fast-Paced Editing: hook-driven content designed to capture attention in the first three seconds.
  • Dynamic Assets: motion graphics, text overlays, and subtitle integration.
  • A/B Testing Versions: multiple iterations (different CTAs or hooks) for performance marketing.
Video, Photography & Crew
  • Motion Graphics: animated logos, kinetic typography, and graphic transitions for social media and advertisements.
  • Video Editing: professional post-production including colour grading, audio mixing, subtitle integration, and platform-specific pacing.
  • Presentation / Pitch Deck: professional slide design for investor pitches, company profiles or sales decks, with custom iconography and data visualisation.
  • Corporate Video: narrative development, professional cinematography, audio/voiceover, and B-roll capture of office environments and facility operations.
  • Product Video: macro/detail shots, lifestyle integration, product motion graphics, and 360-degree views for e-commerce catalogues.
  • Photography / day: full-day coverage (portraits, lifestyle, commercial), professional lighting, retouching, and high-resolution deliverables (RAW + JPG/TIFF).
  • Videography / day: skilled camera operator for b-roll, event footage or interviews, with a standard equipment package and rapid-turnaround field management.
  • Multi-camera: live vision switching, timecode synchronisation, complex coverage of panels and conferences, and centralised audio routing.
  • Drone: regulatory compliance and flight planning, aerial cinematography, and safety protocols with spotters and site risk assessments.
  • Studio Coordination: logistics management, prop and set management, and on-site liaison between client and creative crew.
Post-production
  • Editorial Assembly: pacing, rhythmic editing, and narrative structure.
  • Cinematic Colour Grading: adjusting colour temperature, contrast, and saturation to establish a specific look and mood.
  • Sound Engineering: audio cleanup, background noise removal, music sourcing/licensing, and SFX layering.
  • Motion Graphics / VFX: logo animation, lower-thirds, kinetic typography, and basic visual effects.

Events & Activations

Event Management · Activations · Launches · Conferences
  • Event Management: end-to-end planning (budget, timeline, vendor coordination), site management and floor plan design, and post-event reporting with attendee analytics and budget reconciliation.
  • Brand Activation: concept development, installation design (physical structures, interactive kiosks, photo booths), and consumer engagement through games, sampling and demos.
  • Experiential Marketing: immersion strategy across lighting, sound, smell and touch; digital integration with AR/VR and touch-screen tech; and guerrilla pop-up experiences in high-traffic areas.
  • Product Launch: theatrical production (stage, lighting, AV, scriptwriting for the reveal), influencer and media management, and launch-day hype content.
  • Corporate Event: gala and dinner planning, award ceremony management with scripting and teleprompter services, and VIP handling.
  • Conference: content and speaker management, registration systems with digital ticketing and badge printing, and technical support for AV, Wi-Fi and live streaming.
  • Roadshow: logistics and routing across multiple cities, uniformity of brand look and feel, and local permitting.
  • Trade Show: booth design and fabrication to expo dimensions, collateral production, and scan-to-capture lead generation.
  • Event Staffing: role profiling, pre-event training and briefing, and uniform management.
  • Manpower / day: operational staffing billed per person per shift, performance monitoring, and on-call substitute logistics.

Fabrication

Booths · Stages · Exhibits · Signage · Lights & Sounds
  • Booth Design: space planning and traffic flow analysis, high-fidelity 3D rendering so clients can walk through before production, and branding integration mapped onto the structure.
  • Booth Fabrication: custom build of counters, display walls, shelving and cabinets; material sourcing (MDF, aluminium framing, plexiglass); and hardware integration (locks, hinges, monitor mounts, power ports).
  • Stage Construction: structural planning with load-bearing calculations and safety inspections, professional build-out (risers, decks, staircases, ramps), and finishing with skirting, carpeting and flooring overlays.
  • Exhibit Fabrication: interactive displays and product viewing stations, modular components that transport and assemble quickly, and detailed finishes including paint, vinyl wraps and integrated lighting.
  • Signage: directional and wayfinding signs, promotional foam-core, sintra-board or acrylic displays, and professional mounting and installation.
  • Large Format Printing: high-resolution printing on tarpaulin, vinyl, canvas or back-lit film; backdrops, floor decals, wall murals and hanging banners; hemming, eyelets, pole pockets and UV lamination.
  • Event Styling: conceptualisation of a look and feel (minimalist, rustic, luxury, futuristic), prop curation, and on-site art direction of furniture and decor.
  • Lights & Sounds: lighting design (stage wash, spotlights, mood lighting, moving heads), professional PA systems and wireless microphones, and dedicated engineers throughout the event.
  • LED Wall / day: indoor/outdoor panel rental (P2, P3, P4 pixel pitches), content deployment by professional technicians, and on-site maintenance for power and signal stability.

Music & Audio

Jingles · Recording · Voice-over · Mixing · Original Music
  • Jingle: creative composition of original melodies, hooks and lyrical concepts; arrangement of instruments, beats and backing tracks; and sonic branding — a musical logo that makes the brand instantly recognisable.
  • Recording / hr: soundproof studio time with professional microphones, pre-amps and monitoring gear; on-site engineering; and session management capturing raw audio at professional standards (24-bit/48kHz).
  • Voice-over: casting and auditioning professional voice talent (accents, tone, age ranges), direction on pacing and emotional delivery, and file clean-up removing breath noise, clicks and hums.
  • Mixing: level balancing, frequency management (EQ and compression), spatial effects (reverb, delay, stereo widening), and mastering to loudness standards for TV, radio or streaming.
  • Original Music: custom scoring that avoids copyright issues (royalty-free/exclusive rights), live or high-quality virtual instrumentation, and full sync rights cleared for the client.
  • Talent Coordination: booking and rate negotiation for singers, voice actors and session musicians; studio logistics and technical riders; and rights, releases and usage agreements.

PR & Communications

Retainer · Media Relations · Press · Campaigns · Crisis
  • PR Retainer: monthly strategic counsel aligning PR to business goals, media sentiment monitoring and reporting, proactive reputation management, and continuous media list maintenance.
  • Media Relations: pitching editors, journalists and producers; relationship management that positions the client as an industry expert; interview preparation with briefing documents and Q&A prep; and desk-side briefings with key outlets.
  • Press Release: AP-style drafting, distribution through wire services and targeted lists, multimedia integration to increase pick-up rates, and journalist follow-up.
  • Press Event: media invitations and RSVP tracking, on-site press desk management and press kits, production optimised for media coverage, and post-event follow-up with photos and transcripts.
  • PR Campaign: campaign strategy defining the hook and media mix, asset creation including themes and landing pages, a multi-channel launch timeline, and outcome evaluation against pre-defined KPIs.
  • Crisis Communications: vulnerability assessment, a crisis playbook of holding statements and protocols, active real-time response, and post-crisis recovery to rebuild trust.

Talent, KOL & Influencer

Sourcing · Celebrity Booking · Campaigns · Casting
  • Talent Sourcing: database access through professional networks, vetting via background checks and engagement-authenticity analysis, and availability screening before a shortlist is presented.
  • Celebrity Booking: direct negotiation with agents, managers and publicists; contract management covering riders, payment schedules and image usage rights; and rider fulfilment (travel, accommodation, catering, security, hair and make-up).
  • Influencer Campaign: strategy and briefing against a defined goal (awareness vs. conversion), campaign rollout from draft to final posting, and real-time tracking and mid-campaign optimisation.
  • KOL Management: long-term relationship building for ongoing ambassadorships, performance auditing of audience response, and community integration at events, webinars and in company content.
  • Talent Casting: audition management for actors, models and spokespeople; shortlisting with high-quality reels and headshots; and contractual execution of service agreements and performance releases.
